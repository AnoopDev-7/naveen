About FoodFormulix

FoodFormulix is a specialized food formulation and applied food technology studio dedicated to helping brands create innovative, market-ready food products.

We combine deep scientific expertise with commercial insight to develop stable, scalable, and regulatory-compliant formulations across ready-to-eat, functional, fortified, and specialty food categories.

From concept validation to commercial production, we work confidentially with startups, established manufacturers, and food entrepreneurs to transform ideas into successful products.
